# 🎓 Lumi AI - Your AI study observatory

**Every class becomes a constellation.**

🔗 **Live:** [studywithlumi.com](https://studywithlumi.com) · 👥 **55+ active students**

Upload your study materials and Lumi turns them into conversations, quizzes,
flashcards, and review that sticks - a sky's worth of understanding, charted from
your own notes. Instead of generic answers, Lumi works from the exact documents you
give it (your lecture notes, textbooks, and slides) to explain, quiz, and help you
remember what you learn.

## 🎯 Who it's for

Students who want to study smarter: anyone preparing for an exam, working through a
course, or trying to actually retain what they read. Upload what you're studying, and
Lumi becomes a tutor that has read all of it.

## ✨ Features

- **Converse with your materials:** ask anything and get answers drawn from your own uploaded notes, slides, and readings - grounded in what you actually study, not the open web.
- **Quizzes:** generate practice expeditions from any class in seconds, take them in-app, and get scored with a full review.
- **Flashcards:** turn your materials into flashcard decks in a click.
- **Review that returns:** spaced repetition brings each card back just before you'd forget - on schedule, like clockwork orbits - so what you learn actually sticks.
- **Talk:** a hands-free voice companion for thinking out loud, in a spoken back-and-forth.
- **Classes:** organize your materials class by class and keep your whole sky in one place.
- **History:** revisit your past conversations and study sessions anytime.
- **Support:** built-in help whenever you get stuck.

## 📖 How to use it

1. **Sign in** with Google.
2. **Chart a new class** (for example, "Biology 101").
3. **Upload your materials:** PDFs, notes, and documents for that class.
4. **Converse:** ask Lumi anything, and answers come straight from your uploaded materials.
5. **Generate study aids:** build a quiz or a flashcard deck from your files.
6. **Review daily:** work through the cards that are due and let spaced repetition do the rest.
7. **Talk:** switch to voice for a hands-free, spoken back-and-forth.

## 🧠 Powered by AI

Lumi pairs frontier large language models with prompts purpose-built for studying,
and grounds every answer in your own uploaded coursework - so it explains clearly,
stays on your material, and adapts to whatever you're working on.
